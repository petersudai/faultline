## Summary
This PR prevents compression of Server-Sent Events (SSE) by adding a negative lookahead to the compression regex to exclude text/event-stream. The change is minimal and includes a test, but the test itself has a critical flaw that masks whether the fix actually works.

## Findings (2)

### 🔴 High — src/middleware/compress/index.test.ts:166 — test-gap

The test calls `testCompression('/sse', 'gzip', null)` with `null` as the third argument (expected compression algorithm). Looking at the test setup, `testCompression` likely applies compression middleware and checks the result. Passing `null` as the expected algorithm suggests the test expects NO compression to occur. However, the assertion `expect((await res.arrayBuffer()).byteLength).toBe(13000)` is a magic number with no explanation of how it was derived or why it proves SSE was not compressed. The test does not verify the Content-Type header, does not compare compressed vs uncompressed sizes, and does not confirm the response body is valid SSE format. This test could pass even if compression were incorrectly applied.

→ Check: Verify what `testCompression` does with a `null` third argument. Confirm the test actually validates that the response is uncompressed by either: (1) checking the Content-Encoding header is absent, (2) comparing against a known uncompressed size, or (3) decompressing and verifying the result. The magic number 13000 needs justification.

### 🟡 Medium — src/utils/compress.ts:10 — other

The regex change adds a negative lookahead `(?!event-stream(?:[;\s]|$))` after `text/` to exclude text/event-stream. While the intent is clear, the regex is already complex and difficult to parse. The negative lookahead is positioned to reject 'event-stream' specifically, but the pattern `[^;\s]+` that follows will still match any other text/* type. The fix appears correct for the stated goal, but the regex maintainability is poor and the change increases cognitive load for future reviewers.

→ Check: Manually verify the regex matches text/plain, text/html, text/xml but does NOT match text/event-stream or text/event-stream; charset=utf-8. Consider adding a comment explaining the negative lookahead or refactoring for clarity.
