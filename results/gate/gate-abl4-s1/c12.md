The fix adds a negative lookahead to the COMPRESSIBLE_CONTENT_TYPE_REGEX to exclude `text/event-stream` content type from compression, since CompressionStream doesn't support manual flushing needed for SSE. A test case is added to verify SSE responses are not compressed. The change is minimal, targeted, and low-risk.
