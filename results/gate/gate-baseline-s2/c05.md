## Summary
This PR adds a `basePath` constructor option to Hono and deprecates the `app.basePath()` method. The change refactors the internal `_basePath` field to a private field `#basePath`, updates the constructor signature to accept a `HonoOptions` type, and modifies the `route()` method to directly manipulate `#basePath` instead of calling `basePath()`. Tests are added for the new option.
