## Summary
This PR refactors the Hono constructor to accept a `basePath` option and deprecates the `app.basePath()` method. It converts the private `_basePath` field to a private field `#basePath`. The main risk is that the `clone()` method does not preserve `#basePath`, and the `#basePath` field lacks a default initializer, creating potential for undefined access.
